旅程路線 · The route

五條認識台灣的路Five ways into Taiwan

每趟旅程都從這些主題出發，可以自由搭配：早上在山上喝茶，下午在陶藝工作室拉坏，晚上坐在主廚吧台前用餐。

Each journey follows one or more of these themes. Mix them freely: a tea morning in the mountains, an afternoon at a potter’s wheel, dinner at a chef’s counter.

茶

茶文化Tea culture

阿里山 ALISHAN · 坪林 PINGLIN · 高山烏龍 OOLONG

走進海拔千米以上、雲霧繚繞的茶園，認識烏龍茶的萎凋、揉捻與烘焙，再和在同一片山坡耕耘數十年的茶師，慢慢品一壺茶。

Walk misty tea gardens above 1,000 metres, learn how oolong is withered, rolled and roasted, and sit for a slow tasting with a tea master who has worked the same slopes for decades.

茶園散步Garden walk焙茶體驗Roasting茶席品茗Tea ceremony
山

原住民族文化與自然Indigenous culture & nature

花蓮 HUALIEN · 台東 TAITUNG · 太魯閣 TAROKO

在台灣東海岸健行穿越大理石峽谷，再走進原住民族部落，聽主人分享食物、編織、歌謠，以及他們與土地相連的故事。

Hike marble canyons on Taiwan’s east coast, then spend time in Indigenous communities whose hosts share food, weaving, song and the stories that tie them to the land.

峽谷健行Gorge hikes部落住宿Village homestay獵人步道Hunter’s trail
食

美食與主廚餐桌Food & the chef’s table

台北 TAIPEI · 台南 TAINAN · 早市 MORNING MARKETS

從早市採買開始，和在地廚師一起下廚，晚上到主廚吧台，品嚐台灣食材做成的精緻料理。台南老城區的小吃，值得專程留一整天。

Start at a morning market, cook with a local chef, and finish at a chef’s counter where Taiwanese ingredients meet fine dining. Tainan’s old-city snacks are a day of their own.

逛市場Market walk料理課Cooking class主廚吧台Chef’s counter
藝

工藝與設計Craft & design

鶯歌陶瓷 YINGGE · 三峽藍染 SANXIA · 台北建築 TAIPEI

在台灣陶瓷重鎮鶯歌拉坏，到三峽體驗藍染，再透過建築讀懂台北：從日治時期的街屋，到新的設計地標。

Throw clay in Yingge, Taiwan’s ceramics town, dye cloth in Sanxia indigo, and read Taipei through its buildings, from Japanese-era shophouses to new design landmarks.

陶藝工作室Pottery藍染體驗Indigo dyeing建築散步Architecture walk
語

華語沉浸Mandarin immersion

台北 TAIPEI · 學生團專屬 FOR STUDENT GROUPS

上午上華語課，下午到城市裡出任務：在夜市點餐、向路人問路、訪問店家。學生當天學到的華語，當天就用得上。

Morning Mandarin classes followed by afternoon missions in the city: ordering at a night market, asking for directions, interviewing shopkeepers. Students use the language the same day they learn it.

私人旅程範例 · Sample private journey

從巷弄到山林，6 天Lanes to Mountains, 6 days

這是把各個主題串在一起的一個例子。每趟旅程都會依你的日期、步調和興趣，從頭開始規劃。

One example of how the themes fit together. Every journey is planned from scratch around your dates, pace and interests.

範例行程 SAMPLE ITINERARY · 依每位旅人調整 ADJUSTED FOR EACH TRAVELER

  1. 第 1 天
    DAY 1

    抵達台北Arrive in Taipei

    機場接機、入住飯店，傍晚散步大稻埕的老茶行街區。

    Airport pick-up, check in, and an evening walk through Dadaocheng’s old tea merchant lanes.

  2. 第 2 天
    DAY 2

    市場與主廚餐桌Markets and a chef’s table

    上午和在地廚師逛早市，中午動手做料理，晚上坐進主廚吧台。

    Morning market with a local cook, hands-on lunch, and dinner at a chef’s counter.

  3. 第 3 天
    DAY 3

    陶土與藍染Clay and indigo

    在鶯歌上一堂陶藝課，下午到三峽老街體驗藍染。

    A pottery session in Yingge, then indigo dyeing in Sanxia’s old street.

  4. 第 4 天
    DAY 4

    東海岸到太魯閣East coast to Taroko

    搭火車沿太平洋海岸南下花蓮，下午走進太魯閣峽谷。

    Train down the Pacific coast to Hualien and an afternoon walk into Taroko Gorge.

  5. 第 5 天
    DAY 5

    與原住民族主人相處With Indigenous hosts

    在部落度過一天：一起用餐、體驗編織，聽長輩說故事。

    A day in an Indigenous community: shared meal, weaving, and stories from the elders.

  6. 第 6 天
    DAY 6

    喝一壺茶，再回家Tea, then home

    前往坪林茶園，和茶師進行最後一場品茗，再送你搭機。

    Pinglin tea gardens and a final tasting with a tea master before your flight.
